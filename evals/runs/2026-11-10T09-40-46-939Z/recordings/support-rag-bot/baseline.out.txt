**Key design points**

- **Scale is modest.** 5,000 questions/day is about 0.06 req/s on average, with maybe a few req/s at peak. One stateless service plus a managed vector/search index is enough. No queues or autoscaling clusters are needed on the query path.
- **Hybrid retrieval.** Keyword search catches exact product names and error codes, and vectors catch paraphrases. A reranker then picks the top few chunks.
- **Handoff is a first-class path.** The `Confidence & Citation Check` step routes to a human when retrieval is weak, the answer can't be tied to sources, or the user asks for a person. The agent receives the transcript and retrieved context.
- **Ticket data is scrubbed before indexing.** Resolved tickets carry customer PII, so they pass through the scrubber before chunking.
- **Closed loop.** Agent resolutions of handed-off tickets flow back into the corpus on the next ingestion run, so the assistant improves on the questions it couldn't answer.
- **Freshness.** Articles re-index on publish, and tickets re-index on a nightly schedule.

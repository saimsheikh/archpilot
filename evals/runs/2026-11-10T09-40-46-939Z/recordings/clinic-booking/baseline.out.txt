**Key design decisions**

- **EU residency:** Every component, backup, key and log lives in an EU region. The CDN/WAF is restricted to EU edge locations, and the SMS and email vendors are EU-based with data processing agreements.
- **PHI isolation:** Intake answers are kept in a separate store, with field-level encryption and keys in an EU KMS. Booking data (who, when, where) is kept apart from health data, so a breach of one does not expose the other.
- **SMS carries no health data:** Reminders contain only the time, clinic and a link. Sensitive details stay behind login.
- **Event-driven reminders:** Booking events go through a queue, so the notification service can schedule and retry SMS sends without slowing down booking.
- **Audit trail:** Reads and writes of intake data are logged to an append-only audit store, which supports GDPR accountability. Application logs are scrubbed of PHI.
- **Slot consistency:** The booking service reserves slots through the scheduling service with DB-level locking or unique constraints. Redis only speeds up availability reads, and the database remains the source of truth.

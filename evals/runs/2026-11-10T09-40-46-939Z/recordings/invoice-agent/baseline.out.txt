**Key design points**

- **Human approval gate:** the agent only drafts entries. Nothing is written to the accounting system as a payment until the accountant approves, and the adapter is the only component that writes there.
- **Confidence routing:** low-confidence extractions go straight to review instead of matching on bad data.
- **Adapter layer:** isolates QuickBooks, Xero, and similar differences, so the matching logic stays software-agnostic.
- **Per-client config:** tolerances, credentials, and matching rules are stored per client, which fits a multi-client firm.
- **Audit log:** records every agent action and human decision.

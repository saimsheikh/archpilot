**Assumptions I made, since the brief didn't specify:**
- **Raw landing zone:** Raw data is kept before any cleaning, so a bad transform can be replayed without re-pulling from 120 stores.
- **Per-store extraction with retries:** Some POS systems will be offline or late at night. Monitoring flags missing stores instead of failing the whole run.
- **Forecast output:** Forecasts are written back into the warehouse, so dashboards read one place.
- **Weekly forecast:** It runs off the same warehouse, triggered weekly by the orchestrator rather than nightly.

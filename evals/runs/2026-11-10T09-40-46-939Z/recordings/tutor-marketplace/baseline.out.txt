**Key decisions**

- **Modular monolith, not microservices.** The domains are clearly separated, but a small team gets simpler deploys and transactions this way. Modules can be split out later, with payments the most likely first candidate.
- **Payment provider with marketplace support (Stripe Connect or similar).** It handles tutor onboarding, KYC, tax forms, and split payments. The commission is taken as an application fee, so you never hold funds or build your own ledger.
- **Payout flow.** A completed lesson (signaled by a video-provider webhook) triggers a worker job that releases the tutor's share minus commission. Holding funds until the lesson is done also gives you a refund and dispute window.
- **Webhooks go through the queue.** Payment and video events are verified, stored idempotently, and processed asynchronously so retries and duplicates are safe.
- **Search starts in Postgres.** Subject and price filters work with Postgres full-text search and indexes. The separate search index in the diagram is optional until the catalog grows.
